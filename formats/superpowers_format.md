# Superpowers Format: underdog powers

> Theme file. Read **CLAUDE.md first** (general rules: §2 pacing, §3 ingredients, §4 writing style, §4b story logic, §15 titles), then this file. Where they differ, this file wins for this theme.

**Use for:** everyone in the world gets a power or a number, and the narrator's looks like the worst one, until it's the only one that works.
**References (`references/superpowers/`):** Superpower (snail), Parasite (black parasite), Gravity (0.01). See `references/INDEX.md`.

## Quick numbers
- **Length 100–135 s**, narration **3.7–4.2 w/s**. ~390–560 words.
- **Ending is warm, with a joke** that calls back an early line ("Rex walks me to school. Slowly." / "Okay. Float off, Joey.").
- The weak power's hidden ability must be **planted casually in the first third** ("But snails can do one thing.") and used exactly as planted at the climax (§4b.7).
- **Rule of three:** three people (or machines) with better powers try and fail before "Except me."
- Heartfelt endings can slow to ~1.6 s shots in the last 20%.

### Template B — Underdog (Superpower, Parasite)
| Beat | % of runtime | What happens | Example (Superpower) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hook + power system** | 0–15% | "In this world, everyone gets ___." Then 3 quick examples of good powers | "Cheetah kids race… Owl kids do their homework at 3 AM… Eagle kids never lose the ball." |
| **My weak power** | 15–28% | A deadpan reveal, followed by the downsides | "The stone gave me snail." "I'm slow. I leave a sticky trail." |
| **The hidden ability (planted)** | inside the above | One small thing the weak power can do, mentioned casually | "But snails can do one thing. I stick to anything." |
| **Antagonist / stakes** | 28–40% | The bully with the strongest power, or a mystery (missing dad) | "Then there's Rex. Rex is Bull." |
| **Inciting disaster** | 40–55% | "Until…" A trip or night where things go wrong | "Until the class trip to Cliffside Park." |
| **Strong ones fail (rule of three)** | 55–68% | 3 people with better powers try and fail | Eagle girl blown back, Cheetah slips, teacher's rope too short |
| **"Except me."** | 68–70% | Turning point, in one line | "Too slippery for anyone. Except me." |
| **Climax** | 70–88% | The weakness becomes the strength (slow, sticky, absorbs) | "I can't hurry. I'm a snail." / The black parasite absorbs every color |
| **Resolution + kicker** | 88–100% | The world flips, then a one-word or short punchline | "Nobody called me slime again… Rex walks me to school. Slowly." |

Worked examples: `reference_scripts.md` (Superpower, Parasite) and `reference_analysis.md` §8 (Gravity, beat by beat with times).
**Gravity's upgrade:** after the save, a second problem appears, and the other character's "normal" trait solves it. Use it when the climax feels too easy.

## Writing patterns that belong to this theme
- Three quick examples of good powers: "Cheetah kids race… Owl kids do their homework at 3 AM… Eagle kids never lose the ball."
- Deadpan reveal: "The stone gave me snail." / "Mine is black. The weakest." / "I'm a 0.01, the lowest ever."
- Fragment add-ons: "Slow. Sticky. Useless."
- "Then there's [Name]. [Name] is [strong power]." introduces the bully.
- "Until ___." starts the disaster. "Everyone ___. Except me." is the turn.
