# Crush Gravity (idea 17) — Template F2 "Rule Betrays Her", tsundere crush

**Title:** My crush's mood controls gravity 😳
**Theme:** crush (`formats/crush_format.md`), F2 with F's ladder + "Best ___ of my life". Kicker = dry new rule + her last word.
**Twist:** Rose is a tsundere. She never admits she likes him; every line she says is cold, and the gravity says the opposite.

## Rules box
- **Trigger:** how Rose really feels, not what her face or words say.
- **Effect:** happy = everything at school floats (more happy, more float). Sad = everything gets heavy (floors crack).
- **Exception the twist needs (planted at 0:10):** her face lies. Mr Hale: "Never watch her face. Watch the ceiling."
- **What holds:** nothing in the story contradicts the rule. Every cold line from her comes with a float.
