## 1. Breaking the Fourth Wall — Template A: Horror + fourth wall break
**Target:** 130-180s·**Lighting:** day, sunset
**Plot:** This starts off seeming like a normal horror script, but protagonists realizes he's been put in "In this town/In this world" videos countless times in a simulation. This setting is the same as the "pineapple" video setting. Narrator is shortened to "P"

P: "In this town, if someone calls your (Get's cut off)"

(muffled banging and shouting very far in the distance)
(pauses)
**Rules:**
P: "If someone calls your name after dark, don't answer. Every night after dark, the caller walks out of the woods. It walks through the town, calling in the voices of someone you know. Your friends, your family, even your"
(glitches)
P: "dog. It copies anything it hears. If you answer or open the door, you're gone by morning. That's why every family has a secret word. If the voice doesn't say it, don't open the door."

**Who I am**
P: "My little sister Mina is scared of everything, so I'm the one who checks the doors. Our secret word is pine-(glitches) Strawberry. Mina picked it."
slight pause
"Hmm, for some reason I feel like we had a different word at one point. Anyways, we made a promise. Never open the door, not unless you hear Strawberry"
(this is a throwback to pineapple video)

**Escalation** "The Tiller's have a safe word. "butter". They use it to call their cat home every night. Loud enough for the whole street to hear. Old Mr Ortiz always looked down on them, saying 'never say your word out loud. it listens'. For some reason, I get a strange feeling of deja vu."

**Slowly breaking out** "Monday, I had a strange feeling about the Tillers. Mr Ortiz' words sounded very familiar, as if I heard them before. I decided to peak through the window, spying on them."

The caller is standing in front of the Tiller's house, knocking.
"Grandpa Joey. It's me, Cocoa."
P: "Cocoa? I have no idea who that is, nobody goes in their family goes by that name."
Mr Tiller opens the door, holding his mug. He freezes, falls asleep, then the caller starts dragging him towards the woods.

P: "I gasped. I don't know how but I had a feeling that was going to happen. Today. So, I did the one thing nobody had dared to do. I decided to follow it. I slipped out the back, following behind it, hiding behind bushes and trees. Nobody had gone this far out of the town. Actually, I don't think anybody knows what goes beyond this town"
(foreshadowing that this town is fake)

Eventually the caller reaches the end, a giant wall, made to look like the sky. A door opens, P follows caller inside.
Inside, is like the video production studio of these "In this town/ in this world".
P: "hundreds of screens across the walls. one showed me in my house, me in a town, one where I hatch an egg, my sister is drawing, one with a girl they called my crush. Many places I didn't recognize. Some weren't even me. No, there were hundreds of different kids, living in different worlds."

P:"Then I heard sounds from the screens:
"In this world, snowmen get closer", "In this world, there's a theme park", "In this world, I Got the Weakest Voice".
All chanting the same thing."

Then red lights flash, on the intercom a voice:
"CODE 2: BREACHED CONTAINMENT. AREA 45."

Security guards, film directors, and scientists in white robes rush in. Protagonist gets tackled.
Security: "Subject F-13 Has been contained!"
Film director: "How is this the second time Area 45 got breached again!? F-12 breached this morning, and now F-13 too?"

P: "Then I remembered. The banging, the shouting I heard earlier today. that was... whatever F-12 was."

Film Director: "and double the dose you give F-13. Make sure he doesn't remember ANY of this."

One scientist rushes over with vial, and drops a few drops of a purple substance from it into his mouth.

P: "I tried to scream and kick, but nothing worked"

Film Director: "Send in F-14 before the town notices he's gone, and triple the security guards watching!"

P: "My eyes started to get very heavy, My mind started numbing, thoughts blurry. Right before I closed my eyes, I looked at the vial. On it was one word: "Siratonin"."
(this is a reference to the channel name, pronounced serotonin).
