## 1. Breaking the Fourth Wall — Template A: Horror + fourth wall break (v3)
**Target:** 130-180s · **Lighting:** sunset at the start, night from the rules onward (the town goes dark when the rules are explained; the studio is lit red)
**Plot:** This starts off seeming like a normal horror script, but the protagonist realizes he's been put in "In this town/In this world" videos countless times in a simulation. This setting is the same as the "pineapple" video setting. Narrator is shortened to "P".

**Backstory (never said in full; the studio lines carry it):**
- The studio films every "In this town / In this world" story as **takes**, over and over, until one does well, like a real movie. At the end of every take everyone in it is drugged with Siratonin so they forget and can go again.
- P is **F-13** and has been through this town before (the Pineapple take, among others). The dose never wipes everything, so his own old memories leak: his old secret word, Mr Ortiz's saying, "Cocoa". That is his déjà vu.
- **F-12** is a clone of the same kid (same face, same role), the subject of the take before P. His Siratonin from the previous take wore off, so he remembered, broke out this morning and reached the studio. He was caught and dosed again. **While he was being dosed he screamed, and that is the far-off banging and shouting P hears in the cold open.** The take was ruined (he saw the studio), so they put F-13 in to start a fresh one.
- Why the town never notices: everyone else is dosed after every take too, so only P, who has been through the most takes, builds up leftovers.

**Rules box:** Trigger: someone calls your name after dark. Effect: answer or open the door and you're gone by morning. The Caller copies any voice it has heard. Exception: it only gets in if it says the family's secret word, because the word was said out loud somewhere it was listening. Mr Ortiz: "Never say your word out loud. It listens."
